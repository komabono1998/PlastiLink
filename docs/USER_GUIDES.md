# Short user guides

One page per role. Print them for hubs. Screens use plain language; if a screen is not shown to you, your role does not need it.

## Waste picker
- **Register:** open the PlastiLink address, tap *Register as a waste picker*, fill in your name, phone, neighbourhood and a password, accept the consent. A hub manager will approve you; you can then sign in with your **phone number** and password.
- **After each delivery** the hub weighs your plastic and records it. When a second person approves it you get a **receipt** (*My collections*) and the money appears in *My earnings*. You may also get an SMS.
- **Getting paid:** *My earnings* → *Request a payout*. Choose cash (at your hub) or mobile money (to your registered phone). Someone else must approve it, then the hub pays. You can cancel a request that is still waiting.
- **USSD** (if your programme has switched it on): dial the programme's code for balance, last collections, prices and a payout request. A payout request by USSD is small, limited per day and still needs approval.
- **Your safety:** never share your password. Staff will never ask for it. Sign out on shared phones.

## Hub operator
- **Record a delivery:** *Record collection* → find the picker (name, code or phone) → choose the plastic → enter **gross** and **tare** weights from the scale → check the net weight and value → *Save*. Tap once: the form protects against double saves.
- Mistake before approval? Open it and *Reweigh*. After approval, ask the hub manager (reversals need a reason and leave a record).
- You cannot approve your own entries. Tell the hub manager there is something pending.

## Hub manager
- **Daily:** *Approvals* (new pickers) and *Collections* (pending deliveries) → approve or reject with a reason.
- **Stock:** *Inventory* shows what the hub holds. *Transfers* move stock between hubs (it counts only when the receiving hub confirms). *Dispatches* send stock to a recycler's order. *Adjustments* record losses or corrections and need a second approver.
- **Payouts:** approved requests appear as payments to pay; record the cash receipt note or mobile money transaction ID.
- **Reports and map:** *Reports* (collections, pickers, hub performance, inventory, geographic) for **your hubs only**; *Map and hotspots*.

## Recycler
- **Register** your company; the PlastiLink team approves it.
- **Marketplace:** see available stock, place an order (quantity and price). The hub may counter-offer; accept or counter within the offer window.
- **Receiving:** when a load arrives, confirm the kilograms you actually received. If a load is short, say so: you are **invoiced only for what you confirmed**.
- **Money:** *Invoices* and *Account statement* show what you owe. Payments you make are recorded by staff and confirmed by a second person.

## Project manager
- Approve **payout requests** (never your own), record and reconcile recycler payments, open all **reports** and download CSV/Excel/PDF, follow **Programme progress** (targets and monthly figures; only the Super Administrator edits targets).

## Super administrator
- Create and suspend users; reset a password (a temporary one is shown once); set neighbourhoods (with coordinates), hubs and staff assignments; set **prices**; change **programme targets**; read the **audit trail**; check **Finance** and **Inventory** ledger checks weekly. See `docs/OPERATIONS.md`.
