## Replacing them with your own photos

Real photos of Gulu's pickers and hubs are better. Take them with permission (ask everyone who is recognisable), save them over the files above (JPEG, about 1100 px wide for `gulu-monument.jpg`, 760 px for the others, under 200 KB each), and update the credit line at the bottom of `resources/views/home/index.php` and this file.
